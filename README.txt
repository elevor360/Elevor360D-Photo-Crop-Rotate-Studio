Elevor360D Photo Crop & Rotate Studio V1

Upload all extracted files and folders to a GitHub Pages repository.
Enable Pages in Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch.

Included:
- index.html: image editor and advertising carousel
- manifest.webmanifest, icon.svg, sw.js: installable offline PWA
- images/elevor360d-logo.png: brand logo
- advertising/adverts.json and advertising/images/: rotating adverts

Drag over the image to choose a crop area. Use Apply Crop, Rotate, Flip, Undo, Reset Original, and Download Edited Image.

For new adverts, place pictures in advertising/images/ and edit advertising/adverts.json. Keep paths relative to the app root.

All image editing takes place locally on the device. Some large images may exceed device memory.
